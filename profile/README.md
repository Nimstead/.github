We build **Nimstead Assure**: vulnerability scanning, patch assurance and audit evidence for IT and security teams who have been oversold before.

**It tells you what to fix first.** Not 4,000 CVEs. "Upgrading openssl fixes 43 vulnerabilities on 1 host." Ranked by what attackers are actually exploiting (CISA KEV, EPSS), with the reason written next to every score.

**It fixes things without surprises.** You approve the change. It goes to canary machines first, checks your services stay healthy, and rolls back on its own if they don't. A fix only counts once the next scan shows the vulnerability is gone.

**It proves the work.** Every detection, fix and decision goes into a tamper-evident record. You get a signed evidence pack for Cyber Essentials, ISO 27001 A.8.8, NIST, PCI DSS and CIS that your auditor can verify offline, without asking us.

### Where we are

In private development. Detection, fixes and rollback are proven end to end on real machines: Debian, Ubuntu, AlmaLinux, Alpine, openSUSE and Windows Server 2025.
